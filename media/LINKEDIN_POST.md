Built in one hour at ITCHATHON: Sapient.X. Powered by AGI Corp.

Small businesses already detect their problems. The camera flags the aisle. The dashboard shows the return rate. The POS has a forecast. What nobody gives the owner is the next step, in plain words, in under a minute, alone.

Sapient.X takes four signals and returns one action each:
• Returns: one cause per SKU, the buyer's own words as proof, a fix you can paste into the listing today
• Prep: one number per item at 7am, and you choose whether you'd rather run out or have leftovers
• Ads: an honest "no winner yet" when the numbers can't support one, then what to change anyway
• Theft: the sentence to say in the ten seconds after an alert. No confrontation. No model.

How it was built: no product prompt was written by hand. A meta-prompt writes each version, a team of agents runs it against labelled cases, and nothing ships unless it passes the eval gate. Returns went from 78% to 98% in one loop. Then we ran it on real Amazon reviews and it scored 40%, so that's the next loop, and we're saying so.
