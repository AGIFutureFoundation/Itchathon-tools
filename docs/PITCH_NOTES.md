# Pitch notes — Owner Console

## Thesis
Detection is solved; the next step is not. Every small shop already has data that says
what went wrong. Nobody has told the one person standing there what to do next.

## Four modules, one line each
- **Returns:** paste the listing and the returns; get the one cause, quoted evidence, and a fix you can paste.
- **Prep:** before the rush, before the audit, before the season — the checklist built from your own data.
- **Ads:** the one listing or ad change worth making this week, with the number that justifies it.
- **Ten Seconds After:** the camera fired; here is the sentence to say and where to stand, no confrontation, in under a second.

## Three demo beats
1. **The returns diagnosis.** Ben's hoodie has 14 returns. Paste. Ten seconds later: `size_chart`,
   three verbatim quotes from three different sizes, and the size-chart line to change. Then show
   `_meta.prompt_source: v3.md` and the eval score in the prompt header — that answer was tested
   before it was allowed to appear.
2. **The theft alert.** Veesion flags the beauty aisle, $10 item, one person on the floor, repeat
   visitor. The screen fills with one sentence in 42pt type and a ten-second ring. No model, no
   delay, no accusation. Log "deterred". The monthly table updates: is it worth moving that shelf?
3. **The platform under it.** Same request with `Authorization: Bearer demo-key-kitchen` — the
   theft module is refused (not on that plan), the rate limiter counts, and `data/audit/<today>.jsonl`
   has a row with tenant, prompt version, model, latency and a hash of the input. Nothing else.

## Close
We do not sell detection. We sell the next ten seconds, and a record of what happened in them.
